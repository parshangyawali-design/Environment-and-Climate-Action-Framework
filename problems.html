PRACTICAL EXAMPLES

What to look
for first.

Digital observation is a useful start—not a local diagnosis. Verify findings before publicizing claims or sending people to investigate.

01

Extreme heat

Map official forecasts and shade data; ask residents where cooling, water and safe routes are missing.

Smoke and air quality

Check validated local monitoring and health-authority alerts. Never approach a fire or hazardous plume to investigate.

Habitat loss

04

Flood risk
